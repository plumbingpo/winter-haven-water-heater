Who We Are

Simple Promises, Kept

Winter Haven Water Heater is a local water heater service for houses, apartments, and commercial buildings across the area. We repair gas and electric tanks, install and service tankless units, set up heat pump water heaters, and handle the urgent calls, like a flooded utility closet or a house with no hot water.

Three rules guide every visit: diagnose before we recommend, price before we pick up a tool, and test before we leave. When a repair makes sense, we say so, even if a new heater would be a bigger ticket. For replacements, we look at first-hour rating, flow rate, fuel type, and venting so the new heater fits your home instead of the one on sale.

We work in this community every day, so every job is a chance to earn a referral. It's a simple way to run a business, and it works: do good work, be honest, and people call back.
