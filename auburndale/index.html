How Auburndale Homeowners Can Extend Water Heater Life

The best way to avoid a surprise leak is routine maintenance. We recommend that homeowners flush the tank once a year (more often with hard water), have the anode rod checked every three to five years, test the relief valve annually to make sure it isn't stuck, and keep the thermostat around 120 degrees, which reduces the risk of scalding and slows mineral buildup. Tankless owners should plan on a descaling flush once a year if their water is hard.

How old is your water heater? The serial number on the label usually includes the month and year it was built, and that date tells you a lot. Most tank heaters make it to 8 to 12 years, heat pump units 10 to 15, and regularly descaled tankless units around 20. If your heater is past that range and is also showing rusty water, popping sounds, or dampness under the tank, it's a strong candidate for a planned replacement, which is almost always cheaper and less stressful than a rushed emergency install once the tank fails.

The sooner you call about a hot water problem, the smaller the repair tends to be. A sediment layer that's just starting to rumble can often be fixed with a flush, yet the same sediment, neglected through a few more years, can create hot spots on the tank floor and burn out the lower element on an electric model, turning a simple visit into a replacement. Save our number in your contacts today so help is one call away as soon as something sounds or looks wrong. Early intervention saves time, money, and cold showers.

Hot Water Headaches Auburndale Homeowners Face

Every type of water heater has its own weak points, and we work on all of them around Auburndale. Gas tanks usually give trouble through pilot assemblies and thermocouples, sooty burners, and draft problems in the flue. Electric tanks burn out heating elements and thermostats, especially when sediment buries the lower element. Tankless units throw error codes for scale buildup, ignition failures, and flow sensor trouble, most of which trace back to skipped descaling. Hybrid heat pump units need clean air filters and a clear condensate drain, and they struggle in cramped closets. We service all four types regularly and carry the parts to fix most problems on the first visit.
