How to Tell Something Is Wrong

Catching a symptom early is often the difference between a quick part swap and a tank that gives out on the garage floor. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Dish or hand sink temperatures drifting below what your inspector expects
  • Distant fixtures that take minutes to run warm
  • Scalding bursts or wild temperature swings at showers or sinks
  • Lukewarm water at the exact time you need the most
  • Energy costs climbing while usage stays flat
  • A commercial heater that has grown noticeably louder
  • Equipment approaching the end of its expected service life
  • Visible corrosion or water pooling near the tank

Why This Job Calls for a Pro

Scaling up a water heater changes almost everything about it. Higher gas input means the supply line, meter, combustion air, and vent all have to be sized for the load. Simple Ways to Extend Your Water Heater's Life

A little routine care goes a long way toward keeping a water heater running well past its expected lifespan. Start with these practical measures our team relies on:

  • Flush commercial tanks more often than residential ones, often every few months in heavy use or hard water
  • Log water temperatures at key fixtures so drift is caught early
  • Plan replacement before an aging unit fails during peak season
  • Verify delivery temperatures at sinks and showers a few times a year
  • Listen for a failing recirculation pump and have check valves inspected
  • Inspect anode rods regularly, since heavy use consumes them faster
  • Do not store supplies against the equipment or block air openings
